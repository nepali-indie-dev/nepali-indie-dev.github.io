Core Features

Any place. Any pace.

Pin a point, draw a route, or replay a recorded track.

Simulate a point

Long-press the map, search a place, or type coordinates, then simulate that location.

Draw a route

Tap waypoints on the map, drag them to adjust, and play the route.

Import GPX and KML

Replay tracks, routes and waypoints from GPX files and lines and points from KML files.

Walking to driving speed

Pick a walk, run, cycle or drive preset, or set your own speed and update interval.

Playback controls

Pause, seek, loop, and follow the simulated position from the app or its notification.

Save your routes

Keep routes and favourite places in your library and reuse them anytime.
