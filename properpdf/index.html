Overview

Welcome to ProperPDF, the all-in-one PDF toolkit designed for speed, simplicity, and security. Whether you are a student, a business professional, or managing personal paperwork, ProperPDF provides a complete suite of professional tools to handle your documents without the complexity.

Designed to replace clunky, slow software, ProperPDF offers a clean, reliable, and offline experience. Manage your digital library, edit metadata, secure your files, and convert images with a single tap.

Why Choose ProperPDF?

  • 🚀 Fast & Lightweight: Optimized for performance, ensuring your documents load instantly and processes finish in seconds.
  • 🔒 100% Offline Privacy: Your data belongs to you. ProperPDF processes files directly on your device—no cloud uploads, no data breaches.
  • 🛠️ Complete Toolkit: From merging reports to signing contracts, every tool you need is right here.

Core Features

📄 Merge & Split PDFs

  • Merge PDF: Combine multiple documents into a single, organized file. Perfect for compiling reports, invoices, or study notes.
  • Split PDF: Extract specific pages or separate a large document into smaller, manageable files.

🗜️ Compress & Optimize

  • Compress PDF: Reduce file size significantly without losing quality. Make your documents easy to email and share.

✍️ Sign & Fill Forms

  • Sign PDF: Add your custom E-signature to contracts and agreements instantly. No printer needed.
  • Fill Forms: Easily fill out application forms, tax documents, and surveys directly on your mobile device.

🔒 Advanced Security

  • Lock PDF: Protect sensitive data by adding password encryption to your files.
  • Unlock PDF: Remove passwords from documents you own for easier access and editing.

🖼️ Image & PDF Conversion

  • Image to PDF: Snap a photo of receipts, notes, or whiteboards and convert them into professional PDFs instantly.
  • PDF to Image: Extract pages from a PDF and save them as high-quality images (JPG/PNG) for easy sharing on social media.

📂 Library Management & Editing

  • Edit Metadata: Organize your library by modifying the Author, Creator, Subject, and Keywords of your files.
  • File Management: Built-in viewer allows you to delete, rename, and sort your PDF library efficiently.

Who is ProperPDF For?

  • Students: Merge lecture slides and compress assignments for easy submission.
  • Business Professionals: Sign contracts on the go and encrypt confidential reports.
  • Freelancers: Create invoices using image-to-PDF and organize client files.
  • Everyone: A simple, no-nonsense tool for viewing and managing everyday documents.
