What you can do

Tools marked Pro come with the paid upgrade.

Convert

Turn iPhone HEIC photos into JPG, PNG or WebP, or switch between any of them in a tap.

Compress

Shrink a photo to an exact file size, handy for forms and emails with upload limits.

Resize

Change a photo’s width and height and keep it sharp.

Crop & Rotate

Crop, rotate and flip. Photos that show up sideways are straightened automatically.

Image → PDF

Put several photos into one PDF, sized to fit, A4 or Letter.
