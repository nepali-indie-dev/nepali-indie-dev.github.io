Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective: June 8, 2026 · Last updated: June 8, 2026

Chiranjeevi Pandey ("we," "our," or "us") built ProperImage as a freemium app. This Privacy Policy explains how ProperImage handles your information.

The short version: ProperImage works entirely on your device. We do not collect, upload, sell, or share your photos or personal data.

By using ProperImage, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information.

  • ProperImage has no servers. All image processing — converting, compressing, resizing, cropping, background removal, PDF creation, beautifying, and watermarking — happens locally on your device.
  • We do not require an account or sign-in.
  • We do not collect analytics, usage data, or device identifiers.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • Your photos and the files you create never leave your device through ProperImage.

Photos Access

ProperImage asks for access to your photo library only to:

  • Read the photos you choose to edit (via the system photo picker), and
  • Add edited images you choose to save back to your library.

This access is used solely on your device to perform the task you requested. We do not transmit, store, or analyze your photos off-device.

Support Requests

If you email us for support, we receive your email address and the content of your message. We use this only to respond to you. We do not use it for any other purpose.

2. How We Use Information

Because we do not collect personal data, there is no personal data for us to use, share, or sell.

Support emails you send are used only to answer your question and are not shared with third parties except as required to provide the response.

3. Purchases

ProperImage offers an optional Pro upgrade (subscriptions and a one-time lifetime purchase). Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store and managed with RevenueCat, a subscription-management service.

  • We never see or store your payment card details — Apple handles all payment processing.
  • RevenueCat processes a non-identifying purchase token and an anonymous app-generated identifier solely to validate your purchase and restore it across your devices. It does not receive your name, email, or photos.
  • Apple App Store — Payment processing & purchase delivery — Apple Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat — Purchase validation & restore — RevenueCat Privacy

These are the only third-party services ProperImage uses, and only when you make or restore a purchase.

4. Data Retention

We do not hold your personal data, so there is nothing for us to retain. Your images and settings stay on your device under your control. Support email correspondence is retained only as long as needed to resolve your request.

5. Data Security

Because processing is on-device and we operate no servers, your content is never exposed to network transmission or remote storage by ProperImage. App preferences are stored locally on your device.

6. Your Rights

You remain in full control of your data at all times:

  • Access / deletion: Your images live in your own Photos library and device storage — manage or delete them directly.
  • Purchases: Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple ID settings; restore purchases from within the app.

If you have any request or question, contact us at sublimecoder247@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

European Union (GDPR), California (CCPA), and India (DPDP)

ProperImage does not collect, process, sell, or share personal data, so most data-subject obligations do not apply. Regardless of where you live:

  • Data controller / fiduciary: Chiranjeevi Pandey, sublimecoder247@gmail.com
  • We do not sell or share personal information.
  • We do not use your data for advertising or cross-app tracking.

You may contact us at sublimecoder247@gmail.com to exercise any applicable right or to lodge a concern.

7. Children's Privacy

ProperImage is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children. Because no data is collected, no personal information from children is gathered.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make significant changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date above and post the revised policy at its public URL. Continued use of ProperImage after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, contact us:
