Keep your speakers crystal clear with cutting-edge acoustic technology.

Overview

Speaker Cleaner is a powerful, science-backed mobile app designed to eject water and remove dust from your smartphone speakers using precisely calibrated sound frequencies. Whether your phone took an accidental dip or accumulated dust over time, Speaker Cleaner helps restore your audio quality instantly.

Using advanced acoustic technology, the app generates optimized sound waves that physically displace water droplets and dislodge particles from your speaker mesh—no tools, no disassembly, just pure physics.

Why Choose Speaker Cleaner?

  • 🔊 Water Eject Technology: Uses 165Hz sine wave proven to efficiently displace water from speaker grilles.
  • 🌪️ Dust Removal Mode: Frequency sweep removes dry particles and debris lodged in speaker mesh.
  • 🔬 Scientifically Calibrated: Based on acoustic research to maximize cleaning effectiveness.
  • 💯 Safe & Non-Invasive: No risk to your device—uses only sound and vibration.

Core Features

🌊 Water Eject (Primary Feature)

  • 165Hz Sine Wave: Plays a scientifically optimized frequency for 10-15 seconds to push out water droplets.
  • Synchronized Haptic Vibration: Combines Taptic Engine (iOS) or Vibrator (Android) with audio for enhanced effectiveness.
  • Visual Feedback: Animated waveform visualizer and circular progress indicator show cleaning progress.
  • Automatic Volume Boost: Forces volume to 100% for maximum effectiveness (with user warning).

🌪️ Dust Mode

  • Frequency Sweep: Sweeps from 100Hz to 10kHz using sawtooth waveform to dislodge dry particles.
  • Deep Clean: Helps remove accumulated dust and debris from speaker mesh.
  • Multiple Cycles: Repeat the process for stubborn dust buildup.

🎵 Stereo Check

  • Left/Right Channel Test: Plays audio through individual speakers with voice synthesis.
  • Balance Verification: Verifies speaker balance and functionality after cleaning.
  • Audio Quality Check: Ensures both speakers are working correctly.

📊 Decibel Meter

  • Real-Time Measurement: Monitors ambient noise levels using your device's microphone.
  • Gauge UI: Visual display showing dB levels from quiet to loud.
  • Privacy Focused: No audio recording—only measures sound levels.

How It Works

  1. Select Mode: Choose Water Eject for liquid or Dust Mode for particles.
  2. Position Device: Place speaker facing down for water, or face up for dust.
  3. Activate: Tap the start button and let the app work its magic.
  4. Verify: Use Stereo Check to confirm speakers are functioning properly.

When to Use Speaker Cleaner

  • After Water Exposure: Got caught in the rain? Dropped your phone in water? Use immediately.
  • Muffled Audio: Notice distorted or quieter sound? Water or dust may be the culprit.
  • Regular Maintenance: Use monthly to prevent dust buildup and maintain audio quality.
  • Before Important Calls: Ensure crystal-clear audio quality for meetings or calls.
