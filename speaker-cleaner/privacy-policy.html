Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 6, 2025

Introduction

Nepali Indie Dev ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Speaker Cleaner - Water Eject mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our app.

Information We Collect

Microphone Access

Speaker Cleaner uses your device's microphone solely for the Decibel Meter feature to measure ambient noise levels. We do not record, store, or transmit any audio data. The microphone is used only for real-time sound level measurement displayed on your device.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • No audio recordings
  • No personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • No location data
  • No contact list access
  • No usage tracking beyond anonymous analytics

Anonymous Analytics

We may collect anonymous, aggregated usage statistics to improve the app, including:

  • Feature usage frequency (which modes are most popular)
  • App crashes and performance data
  • Device type and OS version (for compatibility)

This data is completely anonymous and cannot be used to identify you.

Third-Party Services

Advertising (Free Version)

The free version of Speaker Cleaner displays ads through third-party advertising partners. These partners may collect:

  • Device advertising ID
  • IP address
  • Device information

Please review the privacy policies of our advertising partners for more information on their data practices.

In-App Purchases

Purchase transactions are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play Store. We do not have access to your payment information. Please review Apple's and Google's privacy policies for details on how they handle purchase data.

Data Storage and Security

Speaker Cleaner operates entirely on your device. We do not maintain servers that store your personal data. All app functionality, including audio generation and frequency calculations, happens locally on your device.

Children's Privacy

Speaker Cleaner does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. The app is designed for general audiences and does not require personal information to function.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Deny microphone access (Decibel Meter will be unavailable)
  • Disable ads by purchasing Speaker Cleaner Pro
  • Uninstall the app at any time to cease all data collection

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes indicates acceptance of the updated policy.
