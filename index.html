How we work

We’d rather make a few apps you keep for years than lots of apps you forget.

No tricks

We’re independent and self‑funded, so no one is pushing us to grow at any cost. Fair prices, no fake countdowns, no subscriptions you have to fight to cancel.

Your stuff stays yours

Most of our apps work fully offline. If an app doesn’t need your data, it doesn’t collect it. Simple as that.

Fewer, better apps

We’d rather polish one app than rush out ten. We sweat the small stuff: how a button feels, how a label reads, how fast it opens.

Say namaste

Got a question, found a bug, or just want to tell us what you think? We read every email ourselves and usually reply within two days.
