Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective: November 20, 2026 · Last updated: November 20, 2026

Chiranjeevi Pandey ("we," "our," or "us") built Mero Contacts (मेरो सम्पर्क) for Android. This Privacy Policy explains what information Mero Contacts handles and why.

The short version: Mero Contacts has no internet access. Your contacts are read and saved only in your phone's own contacts, and nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. There is no account, no ads, and no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. We never sell your data or use it to track you.

By using Mero Contacts, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

1. Information the App Handles

Mero Contacts does not require an account or sign-in, and does not ask for your name or email. The app does not request the Android internet permission, so it cannot send data over the network itself.

Your Contacts

  • Mero Contacts asks for permission to read and change your contacts. It uses this to show your contacts and favourites, to search them, and to save, edit or delete a contact when you ask it to.
  • Contacts are stored in your phone's own contacts storage, the same place your phone's built-in contacts app uses. A new contact is saved to the Google account that holds most of your existing contacts (or only on the phone if there is none), so it is backed up and synced by Google the same way as contacts you save in other apps. That sync is run by Google under your Google account settings, not by us.
  • Mero Contacts does not copy your contacts anywhere else and does not upload them.

Phone Calls

  • If you allow the phone permission, Mero Contacts places a call directly when you confirm it. Without it, the app opens your phone's dialer with the number filled in.
  • Mero Contacts does not read your call history, record calls, or see who calls you.

Photos

When you add a photo to a contact, Mero Contacts uses your camera or the system photo picker. Only the photo you take or choose is read; it is resized and saved into that contact in your phone's contacts. Camera images are kept in the app's private cache and replaced by the next one.

Sharing a Contact

When you choose to send a contact, Mero Contacts creates a picture card or a contact file (vCard) in its private cache and hands it to the app you pick (for example Viber, WhatsApp, Messenger or SMS). That app then handles it under its own privacy policy. Mero Contacts only shares the contact you chose, and only when you tap send.

Voice Features

  • Reading names aloud uses your phone's text-to-speech engine on the device.
  • Searching or entering a name by voice uses your phone's speech recognition service (usually Google) only when you tap the microphone. The audio is handled by that service under its own privacy policy; Mero Contacts receives only the recognised text.

Settings and Device Backup

Your settings (language, text size, digits, call confirmation) are stored on your device. If Android backup is turned on, Android may include these settings in your Google account backup so they can be restored on a new device. This backup is run by Google, not by us, and we cannot access it.

Support Requests

If you email us for support, we receive your email address and the content of your message. We use this only to respond to you.

2. How We Use Information

We do not receive any information from the app. Everything described above happens on your phone to provide the features you use. We do not sell data, share it with data brokers, show ads, or use it for advertising or tracking.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Google Play — app download and updates. Google Privacy
  • Your phone's system services — contacts sync (your Google account), speech recognition and text-to-speech, used as described above.

4. Data Retention

We hold no data about you. Contacts stay in your phone's contacts until you delete them. Settings stay on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Support emails are kept only as long as needed to resolve your request.
