Contacts stay on your phone

No internet access, no account, no ads, no analytics. Contacts live in your phone’s own contacts.

Big text, Nepali first

Large type and buttons throughout, in Nepali (नेपाली) or English, with Nepali or English digits.

Free

No ads and no purchases. Every feature is free.

Core Features

One tap to the people who matter.

Call, save and send contacts without small buttons or menus.

Favourites with photos

The people they call most, as big photo tiles on the first screen.

Asks before calling

“Call Ram?” with big Yes and No buttons, so a stray touch never rings anyone.

Reads names aloud

Hear the name before calling, in Nepali or English.

Save contacts easily

Take a photo, say the name, type the number. Saved into the phone’s contacts.

Send a number

As a picture card for Viber, WhatsApp or Messenger, or a contact file that saves in one tap.

Find anyone

Search by name, by number in Nepali or English digits, or by voice. Big keypad too.
