What you type stays on your phone

The keyboard has no internet permission, so it can’t send your typing anywhere. It doesn’t save it either.

No ads, no pop-ups

Free

Core Features

Type namaste, get नमस्ते.

Three ways to write Nepali, one keyboard.

Romanized typing

Type Nepali in English letters. Suggestions come from a built-in Nepali word list, so loose spellings still find the right word.

Traditional layout

The Nepali Unicode layout, with shift characters shown on each key. अ + ा gives आ, not अा.

Nepali voice typing

Tap the mic and speak Nepali. Your phone’s speech service turns it into text.

Emoji

A full emoji panel with the ones you use most kept at the front.

Your size, your theme

Short, Normal or Tall keys, light and dark themes, and optional vibration and key sound.

Careful with passwords

Password fields get a plain English layout with no suggestions and no voice.
