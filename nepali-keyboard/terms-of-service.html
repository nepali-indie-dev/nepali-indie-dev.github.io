2. What the App Does

Nepali Unicode Keyboard is an Android keyboard for typing Nepali in Unicode. It offers Romanized typing (for example, "namaste" becomes नमस्ते), the traditional Nepali Unicode layout, an English layout, emoji, clipboard history, smart suggestions for Nepali dates, amounts and units, optional handwriting recognition (Google ML Kit, off until you turn it on), and Nepali voice typing through your phone's speech recognition service. The App is free and contains no ads or in-app purchases.

3. Suggestions and Voice Typing

Romanized and smart suggestions come from built-in word lists, rules and calendar tables, handwriting is recognised by Google ML Kit, and voice typing is provided by your phone's speech service. None of them is always correct. Check your text before you send or publish it, especially for names, numbers and anything important.
