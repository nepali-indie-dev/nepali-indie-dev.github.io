Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective: October 20, 2026 · Last updated: October 20, 2026

Chiranjeevi Pandey ("we," "our," or "us") built Nepali Unicode Keyboard for Android. This Privacy Policy explains what Nepali Unicode Keyboard does with the text you type and the other information it handles.

The short version: Nepali Unicode Keyboard does not have Android's internet permission, so it cannot send what you type anywhere. It does not save your typing, has no account, no ads and no analytics, and we never receive any of your data. The only time anything leaves your phone is when you tap the microphone: your voice then goes to your phone's speech recognition service (usually Google's) to be turned into text.

By using Nepali Unicode Keyboard, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

1. About Android's Keyboard Warning

When you turn on any keyboard that isn't built into your phone, Android shows a warning that the keyboard "may be able to collect all the text you type, including personal data like passwords and credit card numbers." Android shows this for every keyboard, because a keyboard receives every key you press. The rest of this policy explains what Nepali Unicode Keyboard actually does with it.

2. Information the App Handles

Nepali Unicode Keyboard does not require an account or sign-in, and does not ask for your name, email or phone number.

What You Type

  • Keys you press are turned into text and passed straight to the app you are typing in. Nepali Unicode Keyboard does not store, log or upload what you type.
  • In Romanized mode, the letters of the word you are typing are kept in memory only until you finish the word, so the keyboard can suggest the Nepali spelling. Suggestions come from a word list built into the app; nothing is looked up online.
  • Nepali Unicode Keyboard does not learn or remember the words you type.
  • The app has no internet permission. You can confirm this on its Google Play page under "App permissions," or in your phone's app info.

Password and Private Fields

In password fields, Nepali Unicode Keyboard switches to the plain English layout with no suggestions. In password fields and in apps that ask keyboards not to learn (such as incognito modes), it also hides the microphone and does not record recently used emoji.

Voice Typing (Microphone)

  • Nepali Unicode Keyboard asks for microphone permission the first time you tap the microphone key, and listens only after you tap it.
  • Your voice is passed to the speech recognition service on your phone (usually Google's), which turns it into Nepali text. That service may process your audio on Google's servers. Google handles it under the Google Privacy Policy and your Google account settings.
  • Nepali Unicode Keyboard receives only the resulting text, inserts it into the field you are typing in, and does not keep the audio or the text.
  • You can avoid voice typing altogether by not tapping the microphone, or deny the microphone permission in Android Settings.

Settings and Recently Used Emoji

  • Nepali Unicode Keyboard stores your settings (typing style, key height, vibration, sound, app language) and a short list of emoji you used recently, only on your device.

Device Backup

If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include Nepali Unicode Keyboard's settings and recently used emoji in your Google account backup, so they can be restored on a new device. This backup is run by Google under your Google account settings, not by us, and we cannot access it. You can turn it off in your device's backup settings.

Support Requests

If you email us for support (for example with "Send feedback" in the app's Settings), we receive your email address, the content of your message, and the app version, Android version and device model that the app pre-fills in the email. We use this only to respond to you.

3. How We Use Information

We do not receive any information from the app itself. The information described above is used only on your device to type, show suggestions and remember your settings, and by your phone's speech service when you use voice typing. Support emails are used only to answer you.

We do not sell your data, share it with data brokers, show ads, or use it for advertising or tracking.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Your phone's speech recognition service (usually Google) — only when you tap the microphone. See above. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Play — app download and updates. Google Privacy

Nepali Unicode Keyboard contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

5. Data Retention

We do not hold any data about you. Your settings and recently used emoji stay on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Support emails are kept only as long as needed to resolve your request.

6. Data Security

Without internet access the app cannot transmit data. On-device settings are protected by Android's app sandbox, so other apps cannot read them.

7. Your Choices and Rights

  • Turn the keyboard off: in Android Settings → System → Keyboard (the exact path depends on your phone), or uninstall the app.
  • Microphone: revoke the permission at any time in Android Settings.
  • Your settings and emoji history: clear the app's data in Android Settings.
  • Requests: contact us at sublimecoder247@gmail.com or nepali.indie.dev@gmail.com with any privacy question or request. We will respond within 30 days.
